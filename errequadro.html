ErreQuadro

Designing intuitive workflows for a complex B2B SaaS platform handling high-density data.

Role
UX Designer
Context
B2B · SaaS · Data-heavy product
Scope
UX · UI · Design system · Stakeholder collaboration

The challenge

ErreQuadro is a B2B SaaS platform designed for professionals who work daily with large volumes of structured data. The challenge wasn't simplifying the information, but making complex workflows easier to understand while preserving speed, accuracy and analytical depth.

Approach

Rather than reducing functionality, the design focused on improving clarity through information hierarchy, reusable interaction patterns and consistent behaviours. Every decision aimed to reduce cognitive load while supporting expert users in completing complex tasks efficiently.

Helping users navigate complex datasets

Expert users needed to compare large amounts of information without losing context. The interface was designed around high-density tables, clear visual hierarchy and predictable interactions to improve readability while preserving analytical power.

Building a scalable design system

Reusable components, shared interaction patterns and consistent visual rules helped create a scalable design system, improving consistency across the product and simplifying collaboration with developers.

Reducing context switching

Users frequently needed to understand domain-specific terminology while working. A contextual glossary allowed them to access definitions directly within the interface, reducing interruptions and maintaining workflow continuity.

Designing reliable system feedback

Critical actions were supported with clearer system states, contextual feedback and actionable error messages, helping users recover from errors quickly and reducing uncertainty during sensitive workflows.

Collaboration & Accessibility

Throughout the project I worked closely with Product Owners and Front-end Developers to validate requirements and support implementation. Accessibility principles and WCAG guidelines were considered during the design process to improve readability, visual hierarchy and the overall usability of the platform.

Impact

  • Improved readability of complex datasets through clearer information hierarchy.
  • Created reusable design patterns that increased product consistency and scalability.
  • Reduced workflow friction by providing contextual guidance and actionable system feedback.

Reflection

Designing ErreQuadro reinforced the importance of balancing complexity with usability. Rather than removing information, the challenge was to organise it in a way that allowed expert users to work efficiently, confidently and without unnecessary cognitive effort.
